BHUBANESWAR METRO • 18 CURATED ESCAPES

Trails worth skipping weekend screen time for.

Curated outdoor getaways across Bhubaneswar for students at SOA, KIIT, OUTR, AIIMS, Utkal, Silicon, and CVRCE. Real transit routes, student fare benchmarks, and direct Google Maps navigation.

⛰️ Hills & Caves

Khandagiri & Udayagiri Caves

2nd-century BCE rock-cut monastic caves of Emperor Kharavela. Expansive sandstone terraces, ancient inscriptions, and breezy hilltop panoramas overlooking Bhubaneswar.

  • 📍 Corridor: 3.5 km from ITER • 11 km from KIIT • 2.5 km from OUTR
  • 🚌 Transit: Mo Bus Route 11 from Jagamara / Route 16 transfer Fire Station
  • 🌿 Grass Factor: Rough ancient sandstone & hilltop pine breeze

Deras & Jhumka Dam (Chandaka)

Massive water reservoir bordering Chandaka Wildlife Sanctuary. Raw earthen embankments, pure silence away from traffic horns, and dense teak & bamboo forest canopy.

  • 🚌 Transit: Direct bike/auto via Chandaka road or Pitapalli bypass
  • 🌿 Grass Factor: Cold forest reservoir mist & deep bamboo shade

Bindu Sagar Heritage Corridor

The spiritual heart of Old Town. A serene 1.5 km stone-paved stroll around the sacred Bindu Sagar tank, surrounded by 7th–11th century Kalinga temples including Lingaraj and Ananta Vasudeva.

  • 📍 Corridor: 7 km from ITER • 14 km from KIIT • 6 km from Utkal
  • 🚌 Transit: Mo Bus Route 20 / 21 to Old Town Lingaraj Corridor
  • 🌿 Grass Factor: Ancient carved laterite stone ghats & dawn lake reflections
