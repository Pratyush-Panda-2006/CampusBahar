BH-1 TO BH-9 • DESTINATION GUIDE

Trails worth skipping weekend screen time for.

Detailed breakdown of the four major nature & heritage escapes near campus. Pack your water bottle and step out before Monday mid-sems.

🪨
3.5 KM FROM ITER

Khandagiri & Udayagiri Caves

2nd-century BCE rock-cut monastic caves of Emperor Kharavela. Expansive sandstone terraces, ancient inscriptions, and breezy hilltop panoramas overlooking the city.

  • 🚌 Transit: Mo Bus Route 11 (View Bus Route) / Auto ₹15–₹20
  • ⏰ Best Window: 6:00 AM – 8:30 AM before heat escalates
  • 🌿 Grass Factor: Rough ancient sandstone & hilltop pine breeze

14 KM FROM ITER

Deras Dam & Chandaka Canopy

Massive water reservoir bordering the Chandaka Wildlife Sanctuary. Raw earth embankments, complete silence away from campus horns, and dense teak & bamboo forest canopy.

  • 🚲 Transit: Cycle trek or auto (Transport Hacks) ~₹60–₹80
  • ⏰ Best Window: Early morning dawn-to-dusk nature walks
  • 🌿 Grass Factor: Cold forest mist & deep bamboo shade

6 KM FROM ITER

Ekamra Kanan Botanical Lake

A 500-acre botanical sanctuary featuring a serene lake with pelicans, sweeping emerald grass lawns, rose gardens, and wide tree-shaded park avenues.

  • 🚌 Transit: Mo Bus Route 10 (View Route 10) to Nayapalli
  • ⏰ Best Window: 4:00 PM – 6:30 PM sunset breeze
  • 🌿 Grass Factor: Barefoot dew-soaked lawn walking

11 KM FROM ITER

Dhauli Shanti Stupa & Daya River

Historic white peace pagoda atop Dhauli hill overlooking the emerald Daya River basin. Walk the quiet hill path where Emperor Ashoka renounced warfare 2,200 years ago.

  • 🚌 Transit: Auto/Bus (Corridor 20/21) via Kalpana / Puri bypass
  • ⏰ Best Window: 5:00 PM – 6:45 PM golden hour sunset
  • 🌿 Grass Factor: Sweeping valley river breeze
