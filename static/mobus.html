Hosteller Decision Matrix: Shared Auto vs. Mo Bus

🚌 When to take Mo Bus

Peak sun hours (Mo Buses are fully air-conditioned), traveling towards Nayapalli or Railway Station, and solo expeditions on tight budgets. Fares start at just ₹10.

Check Afternoon Heat Index ➔

🛺 When to take Shared Auto

Short quick hops from ITER Gate to Khandagiri Chhak (₹15–₹20 flat), dawn trips before 6:30 AM when bus frequency is light, or returning close to 8:00 PM curfew.

Learn Auto Driver Odia Phrases ➔

🚲 When to use Mo Cycle

Early morning dawn rides to Khandagiri (3.5 km). Dedicated stands exist right near ITER SOA Gate 1 and Khandagiri Square foothills.
